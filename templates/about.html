About ServiceHub

ServiceHub is an online service marketplace designed to connect customers with trusted service providers for various home services. The platform simplifies the process of booking professional help, making it fast, secure, and convenient.

Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to develop an efficient web-based system that bridges the gap between customers and local service providers while ensuring transparency, reliability, and ease of use.

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend: Python Flask
  • Database: MySQL
  • Charts: Chart.js

Future Enhancements

Future improvements may include real-time chat, online payment integration, location-based provider matching, and AI-driven service recommendations to further enhance the user experience.
